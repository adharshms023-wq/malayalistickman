How it works

1

Choose a video

Open any clip from your phone or computer. It stays on your device.

2

Trim and frame

Pick the start point and a length up to 10 seconds, then fill or fit the square.

3

Create

Your browser records a 512×512 clip with its original sound.

4

Add in WhatsApp

Save the clip, then use Create sticker in WhatsApp and add the sound.

Frequently asked questions

Is malayalistickman free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no watermark.

Are my videos uploaded?

No. Everything is processed locally in your browser, so your video never leaves your device.

Can this website add the sticker to WhatsApp automatically?

No. You save the clip here and finish the sticker inside WhatsApp.

Will the sticker have sound?

WhatsApp has been introducing sound stickers, but they may not be available on every phone yet. If you don't see the Sound option, you can send the clip as a video.

Which format is the output?

MP4 where your browser supports it, otherwise WebM. MP4 is the safer choice for WhatsApp.

Can I make Malayalam meme stickers?

Yes. Use any clip you have the right to use, add a Malayalam caption and create the sticker.

About malayalistickman

Malayali Stickman is a small free tool for turning favourite moments into shareable clips with sound. It was built so anyone can prepare funny Malayalam sticker clips in the browser.

Please only use videos you own or have permission to use.

Privacy

Videos you pick are processed entirely in your browser and are never sent to our server. We do not store your files, captions or sticker output. If you share a clip, the app you choose receives it.
